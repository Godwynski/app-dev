# 🎩 Now You See Me

*Now You See Me* is a **mystery-thriller film** about a group of illusionists who pull off bank heists during their live performances and reward their audiences with the money. It’s filled with **twists, deception, and clever magic tricks**.

---

## 🧙‍♂️ Main Plot
A team known as **The Four Horsemen** uses their magic skills to:
1. Perform incredible stage shows
2. Steal money from corrupt figures
3. Outsmart the FBI and Interpol

> *"The closer you look, the less you see."* — *Now You See Me*
